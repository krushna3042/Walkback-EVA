      Where the numbers come from

      DerivedSlope per step, from the elevation grid. Time is distance over a slope-dependent pace (Tobler-style, scaled to suit pace).
      MeasuredThermal inertia and geology units come from orbital instruments in the real build. Here they are sample layers.
      AssumedPace, slope limits, soft-ground slowdown (up to 45%), 20% default reserve. Set these from NASA EVA limits before any real use.

      Return time assumes the astronaut retraces the outbound path. Slope uphill and downhill cost differently, so out and back are not equal.
